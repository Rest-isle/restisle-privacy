# How to Delete Your Rest-isle Account and Data

**Last Updated:** 2 May 2026

This page describes how to request deletion of your Rest-isle App account and the personal data associated with it.

---

## Who can request deletion

Any registered user of the **Rest-isle App** — residents, gate staff, property administrators, and super-admins — can request deletion of their account.

---

## How to submit a deletion request

Send an email to **hello@rest-isle.com** with:

- **Subject line:** `Account deletion request — Rest-isle App`
- **From:** the email address registered on your Rest-isle account
- **Include:** your full name as registered, and the suite or property you are associated with

If you cannot send from your registered email (for example, you've lost access to it), please email us anyway and we will verify your identity through alternative means before proceeding.

---

## What we will do after receiving your request

1. **Acknowledgement** — within 7 working days, we will email you to confirm we have received your request.
2. **Identity verification** — we may ask for one or two pieces of additional information to confirm the request is genuine. This is for your protection.
3. **Deletion** — within 30 days of receiving a verified request, we will delete or anonymise your personal data as described below.

---

## What gets deleted

When we process your account deletion request, the following data is **permanently deleted**:

- Your account credentials (email, password hash)
- Your profile information (full name, phone number, role, property and suite assignment)
- Your push notification tokens
- Your residency history records (room_residents entries)
- Any photos you uploaded that are linked only to your records

---

## What is retained, and why

A small amount of data is **retained for legitimate business and legal reasons**:

- **Parcel records** that you logged or collected, with your name **replaced by an anonymous identifier**, are retained for **24 months** for audit and dispute resolution purposes. After 24 months, the records themselves are deleted.
- **Aggregate statistics** (e.g. "X parcels logged on date Y at this property") that contain no personally identifiable information are retained indefinitely for operational reporting.
- **Records required by Indian law** — for example, billing records under the Income Tax Act or GST Act — are retained for the duration legally required.

After the 24-month parcel retention period and any legal retention period has lapsed, all remaining traces of your account are removed.

---

## Push notifications and contact

Once your account is deleted:

- You will stop receiving any push notifications from the app
- Your email address will be removed from our systems and no marketing or operational emails will be sent to you
- If you reinstall the app, you will need to be re-onboarded by an administrator (you cannot recover the deleted account)
